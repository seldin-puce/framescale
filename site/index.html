Features

A complete restoration pipeline

Turn on the stages you need. They always run in the right order, on the GPU, with a live 2-second preview of the real result before you commit to a render.

01

Deinterlace

Motion-adaptive BWDIF for old TV and camcorder footage.

02

Denoise

NAFNet for camera noise, Real-CUGAN for anime. Four strengths.

03

Upscale

×2 or ×4 with Real-ESRGAN, with a noise-removal slider.

04

Face Fix

Detects faces and restores them with CodeFormer.

05

Interpolate

×2–×4 frame rate with RIFE, aware of scene cuts.

See it before you render

Scrub your clip, render 2 seconds of the exact pipeline with live progress, and compare split or side by side.

Never lose a render

Jobs are written in 4-second segments and journaled. Pause, quit or crash, and the render resumes where it stopped.

Keeps everything else

HDR10 and HLG, aspect ratio, audio (copied bit-exact), subtitles, chapters and metadata all carry over.

Private by design

Your videos never leave your computer. No accounts, no cloud processing, no analytics.

Modern output

HEVC 10-bit, AV1 10-bit or H.264 (ProRes on macOS), with NVENC, AMF, Quick Sync, VAAPI or VideoToolbox when available.

Eco mode

Hold a GPU temperature target or a fixed duty cycle, so long renders stay quiet and cool.

Models

Proven open models, bundled and verified

Every model ships with the app and is pinned by SHA-256. Models without an official ncnn export are converted from the authors' own weights at build time, and checked against their reference output.

Performance

Fast where it matters

A native Vulkan engine (ncnn) with GPU-side custom layers, tuned tiling and two in-flight tiles per GPU.

WorkloadSpeed
Real-ESRGAN General v3 ×4, 960×540 → 4K13.5 fps
Real-ESR AnimeVideo-v3 ×2, 1080p → 4K10.9 fps
NAFNet denoise, 720×576 / 1080p26.8 / 7.1 fps

CodeFormer face restoration≈0.2 s / face

AMD Radeon RX 9070 XT, Linux, Mesa 26.2. Inference only, excluding decode and encode.
