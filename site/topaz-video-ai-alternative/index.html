A free, open-source alternative to Topaz Video AI

Updated November 2026 · 5 min read

Topaz Video AI is a popular commercial video enhancer. If you want the same core tasks, AI upscaling, denoising, face restoration and frame interpolation, without a paid license, FrameScale AI is a free and open-source option that runs on your own GPU.

Where each tool fits

Choose FrameScale AI if you want a free tool, open-source code you can inspect and modify, Linux support, or full control over which open models process your footage. Its models are widely used, well-documented open research models.

A commercial suite may suit you better if you rely on its own proprietary models, plug-ins for editing software, or paid support. Both kinds of tools run locally on your GPU.

Switching in three steps

  1. Install FrameScale AI. Download the installer for Windows, macOS or Linux from the download section and install it. It is free, needs no account, and runs entirely on your computer.
  2. Rebuild your usual pipeline. Turn on the stages you used before: Upscale for enhancement, Denoise for noise, Face Fix for faces and Interpolate for frame-rate conversion.
  3. Compare on your own footage. Use Render 2 s to preview the exact result on a short section and compare it side by side with the original before rendering the whole video.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free alternative to Topaz Video AI?

Yes. FrameScale AI is free and open source and covers AI upscaling, denoising, face restoration, deinterlacing and frame interpolation on Windows, macOS and Linux.

Is FrameScale AI as good as Topaz Video AI?

They use different models, so results differ by footage. FrameScale uses established open models (Real-ESRGAN, NAFNet, CodeFormer, RIFE). The best test is a 2-second preview on your own clip, which is free.

Does FrameScale AI work on Linux?

Yes. It runs on Windows, macOS and Linux, on NVIDIA, AMD, Intel and Apple GPUs through Vulkan.

Does it add a watermark or need an account?

No. There is no watermark, no account and no limit on resolution or video length.
